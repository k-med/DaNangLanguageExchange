Preference

A fast pattern for comparisons, recommendations, and personal taste.

Starter Patterns

Template

[Subject] thích [A] hơn [B]
Literal English

[Subject] likes [A] more than [B]

Practice question Bạn thích [A] hay [B] hơn?

Examples

  • Tôi thích cà phê Việt Nam hơn cà phê Mỹ. I like Vietnamese coffee more than American coffee.
  • Tôi thích phở hơn bún bò. I like pho more than bun bo.

Prompts

  • Bạn thích [A] hay [B] hơn? Which do you like more, [A] or [B]?
  • Bạn thích ăn ở quán nào hơn? Which place do you prefer eating at?
  • Bạn thích học buổi sáng hay buổi tối hơn? Do you prefer studying in the morning or in the evening?

This pattern gives beginners a reliable way to answer with more than one word.
