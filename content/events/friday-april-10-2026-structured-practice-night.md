## What Worked Well

- Around 20 attendees gave the evening a strong balance between energy and manageability.
- The mix of Vietnamese and international members created natural opportunities for both English and Vietnamese practice.
- Small group rotations of two to four people kept everyone speaking more consistently.
- New members integrated easily because the conversation structure gave them a clear way in.
- The atmosphere stayed relaxed, focused, and conversation-driven instead of drifting into a noisy social free-for-all.
- The venue fit the group size well and supported the format better than a tighter or louder space would have.

## Honest Notes

- We started around 30 minutes late, which pushed the evening beyond the original schedule.
- Even so, people stayed engaged long enough that the session naturally extended to around 9:30 PM to 10:00 PM.
- For future sessions, it would help to tighten the opening and first seating process so we can start the first round more cleanly.

## Main Takeaway

This session made one thing especially clear: there is real demand for regular, structured practice.

The strongest part of the night was not just the turnout. It was the quality of the conversations, the steady speaking time, and the way people stayed engaged across both English and Vietnamese throughout the evening.
