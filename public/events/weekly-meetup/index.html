Friday Cafe Language Exchange

A structured Friday night session with English and Vietnamese practice, small-group rotations, and extra vocabulary prompts to help conversations go beyond simple introductions.

Session details

Date Friday, April 24, 2026 Time 7:30 PM - 10:00 PM Venue Check WhatsApp or Zalo for this Friday's cafe location Table focus Balanced starter, conversation, and vocabulary-supported tables Topic Getting to know each other, extending conversations, and using vocabulary prompts to keep topics moving Hosts Daisy

This is the next public session for the Da Nang Language Exchange Club.

Session Format

  • 7:30 PM - 7:50 PM: Arrivals, welcome desk, and seating help for first-time members.
  • 7:50 PM - 8:20 PM: First conversation round with topic prompts and starter vocabulary support.
  • 8:20 PM - 8:30 PM: Break and table rotation.
  • 8:30 PM - 9:00 PM: Second conversation round with more open follow-up questions.
  • 9:00 PM - 10:00 PM: Flexible mixed practice and continued conversation for members who want longer speaking time.

Practical Details

  • Venue: Check WhatsApp or Zalo for this Friday’s cafe location. We rotate between suitable cafes in Da Nang.
  • Host: Daisy.
  • Capacity target: 18 to 24 members so tables stay manageable.
  • Best for: Learners who want a calm, structured session rather than a free-form social meetup.

What To Bring

  • A phone or notebook for new vocabulary.
  • Enough cash or card for your own drink.
  • The weekly topic page from the Topics section if you want to prep in advance.
  • A small notebook if you want to copy the patterns, save new vocabulary, and write three example sentences.

First-Time Advice

If it is your first session, arrive close to 7:30 PM and tell the host. She will place you at a suitable table and make sure you have starter prompts ready.
