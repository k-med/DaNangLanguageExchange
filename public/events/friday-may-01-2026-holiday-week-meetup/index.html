Friday May 1 Holiday Week Meetup

Our Friday, May 1 meetup took place from 7:00 to 9:00 PM at Heaven Coffee. The smaller holiday-week turnout created a relaxed, friendly rhythm for paired practice and small-table conversation.

Session details

Date Friday, May 1, 2026 Time 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM Venue Heaven Coffee, Da Nang Table focus Small-group English and Vietnamese conversation practice Topic Relaxed holiday-week conversation, paired practice, and steady speaking time Hosts Daisy and Kade

Our Friday, May 1 meetup took place from 7:00 to 9:00 PM at Heaven Coffee.

Because it was still the holiday period, the turnout was smaller than usual, but the evening had a relaxed and friendly rhythm. The quieter setup worked well for paired practice and small-table conversations, giving members more time to speak, listen, ask questions, and settle into natural exchanges.

Instead of needing a heavy rotation structure, the night stayed simple and conversation-first. People had space to continue discussions a little longer, help each other with language points, and enjoy a calmer Friday session outdoors.

It was a good reminder that even a smaller meetup can still be valuable when the people who attend are engaged and ready to practice.

Quick Notes

  • The holiday period reduced turnout, but the members who came were engaged.
  • Heaven Coffee worked well for a quieter outdoor meetup.
  • The 7 to 9 PM timing felt clean and easy to manage.
  • Smaller tables gave members more speaking time.
  • The main value of the night was simple, steady language practice.
